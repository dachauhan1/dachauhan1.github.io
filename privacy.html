Chauhan Research — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Chauhan Research is a data-entry app used only by an authorised research team for the doctoral study “Mathematics Education in the Upper Primary Schools: An Exploratory Study” (IASE, Gujarat Vidyapith, Ahmedabad). It is not intended for use by the general public or by children.

What the app stores

  • Research responses copied from paper questionnaires filled by Standard 8 students: answers to the Objective 2 and 3 schedule, gender, school code (district and school type only), date of data collection and, optionally, a roll number used only to find the paper again. Student names are not collected. Each paper gets an anonymous ID such as AGS01-001.
  • Team accounts: the name, e-mail address and sign-in details of each research team member, and a log of who entered or changed each record (for data quality).

How it is used

  • Only for the research study named above: checking data quality and statistical analysis reported in the thesis in aggregate form. No individual student is identified in any report.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • The roll number is never included in exports or reports.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the researcher's own Google Firebase project (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access is restricted by security rules: a new account can see nothing until the researcher approves it; team members can see only the records they entered; only the researcher can see all records, verify them and export them.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the study is completed and examined, and then deleted or kept in anonymised form as required by the university. A team member may ask the researcher to delete their account at any time.
